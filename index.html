What to expect.

Who is this for?

Beginners and career-switchers who want a way into games, 3D or motion. Designers, illustrators and video editors adding a new skill. And anyone stuck in tutorials who never finishes a project.

Do I need experience?

No. Every course starts from zero. You don't need to code or draw. You need curiosity and about six hours a week.

How does online learning work?

Live lessons with your lecturer twice a week, with recordings if you miss one. You get assignments, feedback on your work and a chat with your group. Every course ends with a finished project. Price and payment options depend on the course, and a manager will send you the details.

What do I need to start?

A laptop or desktop and a stable internet connection. The software is free or comes with your course access (Blender is free, for example). We send the setup list before day one.

What happens after the course?

You finish with a portfolio project and feedback from the people who taught it. Then you decide: apply to studios, take freelance briefs or join the next course. We don't promise jobs. We give you the work and the feedback to go and get one.
